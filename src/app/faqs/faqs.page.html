FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

I have a secret-Item stored in Borrowing and I want to protect that in case my friends or family is using the phone.

We, Team Borrowing understands User's privacy. Users can opt for setting 4-digit PIN from "Account" page. It's completely optional. There is a provision to update or remove PIN as well in case User's want to change their preferences.

I have so many items listed in the "Home" tab. I only want to view items which I have borrowed from my friends and family.

Users can go to "borrowed" section of home page to view the items which they have borrowed from other people. To view items which users have given to people, they can use "lent" section of the same page.

I am a Giver. I have so many items listed in the "Home" tab. It's very hard for me to find a specific Item.

The team Borrowing appreciates people with a big heart. Users can use searchbar provided at the top to browse through the items. Searchbar filters the item list by matching the search query to Item name. Alternatively, users can sort the list by using the funnel icon present at the top right corner of the home page. Please note that users of Borrowing can use combination of sorting and filtering too.

I want to remind the other person about the item.

Users can go to "Item Details" by clicking on Item from the list. You can construct your own reminder message with the help of item details and send using one of the numerous social sharing options provided by Team Borrowing to remind them.

The other person is not on any of the social networking options provided.

You worry too much. There is a generic share option provided with "Share" icon. You can use this to connect to other person in every possible way supported by your phone.

I want to edit the information provided earlier for my item.

Users can go to item details and click on "Edit" icon present at the top right corner to change the texual information related to Item. To add/change image associated with the item, Users can avail "Add Image/Change Image" button.

I want to know about Important Items.

Items which are marked with high imporatance in Borrowing are said to be important Items. Users can navigate to "Important Items" using "Star" icon present in the bottom toolbar and go to "Important" section to view Important items.

I missed the expected date for returning/getting my Item. I want to view such items in Borrowing.

These items are known as "Overdue" Items. Users can view these items in the "Overdue" section of the "Important Items" tab.

I want to remove the item from the list now that I have got back/returned the item.

This is also known as "Complete/Done" item. Users can use "Tick" icon which will be shown after Left-sliding the Item-view on the "Home" tab or "Important Items" tab. This will remove the item from "Home" tab (and "Important Items" tab if the i tem was imporatant). These "Complete / Done" items can be viewed in the "Completed" section of the "Important Items" tab.

I want to view the items for which the borrowing transaction is completed.( You either got back or returned the item )

The user can go to "Important Items" tab. and Click on "Completed" section. The "Complete/Done" items will be listed there.

I don't care about my "Completed/Done" item and I want to remove the this particular item completely from Borrowing.

User can use "Trash" icon which will be shown after Left-sliding the Item-view on the "Completed" section of the "Important Items" tab. Please note that, the item once removed completely, can not be made available again.

I want to track my activities in Borrowing.

User can use "Archive" icon which is present in the top-right corner of the "Account" page to view their activities regarding their Items in Borrowing.

You have a bad taste Team Borrowing. I don't like the default color scheme.

User can change the theme for the "Borrowing" app by going to "Theme" section of "Settings" tab.

I am facing an issue which is not listed here.

Please use "Contact us" section in "Settings" tab to write to us. Please be detailed and specific in your query. The team Borrowing will get in touch with you regarding the issue once we receive the issue.

The "Borrowing" app is awesome. I want to know/meet/greet the developer to show the gratitude.

Please don't forget to rate "Borrowing" app which you like so much. You can go to the "About developer" section of "Settings" tab to know about the Developer. And You can greet Or meet the developer on the social platforms, links for which are provided.
