### What is Borrowed??

Borrowed is an app used to manage your lent/borrowed items which you usually forget to get back or return. Sometimes people forget about things they have lent/borrowed and keep searching for it in the wrong places. Borrowed makes users un-forget the borrowing deals. Users can remind the person on the other end of borrowing transaction via social networking /email /message about things they have given or borrowed. 
Borrowed also have a feature to mark items as urgent which will list items that you want back on urgent basis.

### Borrowed is equipped with following features:
* Social Sign ins
* easy Sign up with email
* User Profile management
* Lent Items listing
* Lent Item search
* Urgent Items tracking
* Send Reminder options
* Transaction history
* Theme picker
* Customer support with email
